│ COMMON QUESTIONS                                                                       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Q: Why negative coefficient for ExxonMobil?
A: Likely mean reversion - when XOM drops, oil rallies next (hedge unwinding)

Q: Why is lagged oil negative?
A: Short-term momentum reversal - if oil dropped, it bounces back 2 weeks later

Q: What's the forecast horizon?
A: Best for 1-4 weeks. Markets become efficient beyond that timeframe.

Q: How often should I update the model?
A: Annually is sufficient. Check for structural breaks if major geopolitical events occur.

Q: Can I use this alone?
A: No - combine with fundamental analysis, technical indicators, geopolitical factors.

Q: What about longer-term forecasts?
A: Model is designed for tactical (1-4 week) horizon. Consider VECM/cointegration for longer term.
