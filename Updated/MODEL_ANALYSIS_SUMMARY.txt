   ├─ Method: Fundamental screening + OLS

SECTION 2: FINAL MODEL SPECIFICATION (RECOMMENDED)
================================================================================

MODEL EQUATION:
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OIL_RETURN_t = 0.001344
             + 0.508721 × OIL_SECTOR_INDEX_t                   ***
             - 0.413635 × EXXONMOBIL_t                          ***
             - 0.080868 × OIL_RETURNS_t-2                       **
             + 0.000000 × COMMERCIAL_SHORT_DIFF_t               ***
             - 0.000001 × COMMERCIAL_LONG_DIFF_t                ***
             - 0.000001 × INVENTORY_CHANGE_t                    ***

(*** p<0.01, ** p<0.05, * p<0.10)

VARIABLES (6 Total):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. OIL SECTOR INDEX (Dow Jones U.S. Oil & Gas)
   Economic Rationale: Investor sentiment about oil sector fundamentals
   Effect: Positive - Higher energy stocks predict higher oil prices
   Data Source: Yahoo Finance (daily)

2. EXXONMOBIL STOCK (XOM)
   Economic Rationale: Leading integrated oil company performance
   Effect: Negative - Suggests mean reversion or hedging behavior
   Data Source: Yahoo Finance (daily)

3. LAGGED OIL RETURNS (t-2)
   Economic Rationale: Short-term momentum and mean reversion
   Effect: Negative - Prices bounce back after 2-week lags
   Data Source: NYMEX futures (daily)

4. COMMERCIAL SHORT POSITIONS (change)
   Economic Rationale: Contrarian indicator of hedging sentiment
   Effect: Positive - More shorts precede price rallies
   Data Source: CFTC COT Reports (weekly Fridays)

5. COMMERCIAL LONG POSITIONS (change)
   Economic Rationale: Producer hedging and long positioning
   Effect: Negative - Declining longs suggest lower expected prices
   Data Source: CFTC COT Reports (weekly Fridays)

6. CRUDE INVENTORY CHANGES (weekly)
   Economic Rationale: Supply/demand balance indicator
   Effect: Negative - Inventory builds = lower prices, draws = higher prices
   Data Source: EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report (Wednesdays)
